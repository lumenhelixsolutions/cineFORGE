Features

Source Ingestion

Import PDFs, Markdown, web pages, and scripts. Extract structure, characters, and scenes automatically.

AI Director

Generates shot-level storyboards with camera angles, continuity, and emotional register from your source material.

Shot Storyboard

Visualize every cut before rendering. Adjust timing, transitions, and framing in a unified timeline.

PromptForge

Auto-optimizes prompts for each video model, ensuring consistent style and maximum fidelity.

Multi-Model Render

Route shots to the best engine — Veo, Kling, Sora, Wan2GP, ComfyUI, and more — from one queue.

Post-Processing

Built-in upscaling, frame interpolation, audio sync, and export to MP4 / ProRes.

How It Works

Ingest

Drop in a PDF, Markdown file, or web URL. CineForge extracts structure, characters, and scenes automatically.

Direct

The AI Director analyzes your source and generates a shot-level treatment with camera angles and emotional pacing.

Render

Each shot is routed to the best video model for its tier. Local or cloud — you control the stack.

Deliver

Stitch, add transitions, sync audio, and export in platform-ready presets for YouTube, TikTok, and more.
